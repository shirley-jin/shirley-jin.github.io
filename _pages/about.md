Welcome to my website! My name is Qian Jin (Chinese: 靳茜, pronunciation: jin chyan), friends also call me Shirley. I'm an Assistant Professor in Social Entrepreneurship in School of Economics at Utrecht University. I earned my PhD in social entrepreneurship in Department of Applied Social Sciences at Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Before joining Utrecht University, I was a Postdoc in Department of Public and International Affairs at City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in several fields and often at the intersections of these areas:

- I study **_social entrepreneurship_**, examining its process and outcomes including legitimacy building, resource acquisition and certification;

- I investigate how _**emerging technologies**_ such as AI, NFT, Metaverse and blockchain enable entrepreneurship;

- I employ _**computational**_ and _**experimental**_ approaches to probe into various organizaitonal phenomena. These approaches include but are not limited to natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, field experiment and online experiment.

My research has been published in top-tier journals such as Journal of Management Studies, Journal of Business Ethics, among others.
